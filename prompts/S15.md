# S15 Performance and adaptive bitrate

Depends on: S13. Branch: step/S15-<name>.

Goal: Meet the latency, recovery and thermal targets with evidence, and adapt bitrate to the link.

## Prompt

```text
Follow AGENTS.md. Step S15, branch step/S15-performance. Read docs/findings.md (PERF-05, PERF-08, PERF-10, PERF-11) and the targets in docs/product-requirements.md (RQ-16).

1. Build tools/bench: a script and an in-app debug mode that run a matrix of (rate control CBR/VBR, bitrate, resolution scale, fps) for 60 s each, logging per-frame stage times, queue depth, drops, keyframe latency, battery temperature and thermal status (PowerManager thermal API). Output CSV and a summary table.
2. Adaptive bitrate: a controller on the server that uses send-queue depth and the RTT trend to lower bitrate quickly and raise it slowly, bounded by the viewer's ceiling; tests with simulated queue traces.
3. Apply FPS and scale changes without tearing down the whole pipeline: change at the next IDR where the encoder allows it; otherwise restart behind a frozen last frame.
4. Optional H.265: negotiate through the capability bitmap only when both encoder and decoder are hardware-backed; keep H.264 fallback; compare quality and heat in the benchmark.
5. Profile allocations and GC on a low-end viewer; remove remaining per-frame allocations.
6. Run the benchmark, choose defaults from the data, and write docs/perf-report.md with tables, chosen defaults and caveats. Include the external camera method steps for the owner.
Definition of done: tests green; docs/perf-report.md committed with real numbers (mark any measurement you could not perform); defaults updated; summary per AGENTS.md.
```

## Acceptance criteria

- Median glass-to-glass latency under 80 ms and p95 under 150 ms at 1080p60 on clean 5 GHz on the reference pair (or documented reasons and the achieved numbers).
- Artefact recovery under 500 ms after an induced loss; reconnect after a one-second outage under three seconds.
- Two-hour soak without memory growth or throttling on the reference old phone.

## Owner checks

- Do the external camera latency measurement (240 fps camera, millisecond counter) for at least 30 samples per condition.
